CEFR

Five levels, from A1 to C1

Lessons in every language follow the European language levels (CEFR). At the end of each level, a test shows whether you're ready for the next one.

  1. A1BeginnerIntroduce yourself, order a coffee, talk about your family.
  2. A2ElementaryTalk about the past, travel, go shopping.
  3. B1IntermediateTalk about work, plans and opinions.
  4. B2Upper intermediateDebate, watch films, read articles.
  5. C1AdvancedSpeak freely and with nuance on any topic.
