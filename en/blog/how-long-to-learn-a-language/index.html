How long does it take to learn a foreign language?

It's the most common question. There's no exact answer, but there are realistic numbers to help you plan.

Hours per level

Cambridge estimates these approximate total guided learning hours, starting from zero:

LevelApproximate hours
A2180–200
B1350–400
B2500–600
C1700–800
C21000–1200

These are averages. Independent work (listening, reading, reviewing) can speed things up a lot.

And in months?

With 3 hours of lessons a week plus 2–3 hours on your own, you can reach A2 in roughly 6–8 months and B1 in about a year. Short daily practice beats rare long sessions.

Which language is faster?

The US Foreign Service Institute (FSI) puts Spanish and French among the easiest languages for English speakers, at about 24–30 weeks of intensive study. For Armenian speakers the picture is a bit different, but Spanish pronunciation usually feels easier than French. See the comparison here.

How to go faster

  • Practise every day, even for 20 minutes.
  • Listen to recordings at your level.
  • Start speaking in the first week.
  • Check your level regularly with a test.
