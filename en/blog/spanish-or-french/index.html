Spanish or French: which should you choose?

Both are beautiful Romance languages with shared roots, but learning them feels quite different.

Pronunciation and spelling

Spanish is mostly read the way it's written. It has few vowels (five main ones), and they sound clear. Armenian speakers usually find this pronunciation easy.

French has many silent letters (such as final consonants), nasal vowels and more vowel sounds. At first it's harder to understand by ear and to read aloud correctly.

Grammar

In both languages nouns have gender, verbs change with the person, and there's a subjunctive mood (subjuntivo / subjonctif). Spanish uses more verb forms in everyday speech, while French is harder to spell correctly.

Where they're spoken

  • Spanish: an official language in about 20 countries (Spain and most of Latin America), with about 500 million native speakers.
  • French: an official language in about 29 countries across Europe, Africa and the Americas, with more than 300 million speakers. Armenia is a full member of the International Organisation of La Francophonie, and Yerevan hosted the Francophonie Summit in 2018.

Which to choose

  • Want to start speaking quickly and travel in Spain and Latin America? Spanish.
  • Interested in studying in France, Belgium or Canada, international organisations or French culture? French.
  • Can't decide? Pick the one you like more. Motivation matters most.

Once you learn one, the other gets much easier: they're from the same family. More on that in how to become a polyglot.
