Frequently asked questions

Can I start French from zero?

Yes. Level A1 is for complete beginners, and all explanations are in Armenian.

How do the lessons work?

All lessons are online, right on the Verba School website, from a phone or computer. You can choose group or private lessons.

How much do lessons cost?

Group lessons are 25,000 AMD per month and private lessons are 30,000 AMD per month.

How do I find out my level?

Take our free test. It gives an approximate level, and your teacher confirms it in the first lesson.

Do you give a certificate?

Yes, at the end of the course you receive a Verba School certificate.
